Localized Preparedness Tips

Tailored advice for your registered region

72-Hour Survival Kit

Ensure you have 1 gallon of water per person per day, non-perishable foods, flashlights, and extra batteries. Rotate stock every 6 months.

Family Communication Plan

Designate an out-of-state contact person. Local networks may crash, but long-distance lines often remain open.

Home Hardening

Secure heavy furniture to walls. Know how and when to shut off utilities (gas, electricity, water) safely.

Immediate Survival Steps:

  • Earthquake: Drop, Cover, and Hold On.
  • Fire: Stay low to the ground. Check doors with the back of your hand before opening.
  • Bleeding: Apply firm, direct pressure to the wound with a clean cloth.
